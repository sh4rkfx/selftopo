# Code of conduct

selftopo is about personal health data, and people come to it with different bodies, goals and
levels of experience. Be respectful and constructive.

- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent and ask before you judge.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwanted personal attention, in any channel of the
  project.
- No comments on other people's bodies, weight, health or performance, including numbers they
  share by accident. Point out the accidental share so they can remove it.
- Never ask anyone to share their health data or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block people who do not follow these rules.
Report problems to the maintainer through GitHub (a private security report also works if you
need confidentiality); reports are handled confidentially.
